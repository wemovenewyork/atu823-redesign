Update Mar 4, 2026

FMCSA Medical Certification Changes — what every operator needs to know.

As of February 23rd, 2026, drivers no longer submit ICC medical certifications directly to MVC. DOT physical results now go from the medical examiner straight to FMCSA, who reports to MVC within 28 to 48 hours.
