Recognizing Local 823

Member of the Month.

Each month, Local 823 honors a member who exemplifies what this union stands for — reliability behind the wheel, mentorship in the garage, and solidarity at the table. Nominated by the membership. Selected by the board.

This Month's Member.
[ Photo · Member Headshot ]
01
Honored / May 2026

Marcus Johnson.

Ironbound Operator 18 Years

Driving Newark isn't just a job — it's how I show up for my family and how I show up for the people on my bus. The man behind me at the depot taught me that. Now I try to teach the new operators the same thing. We hold this line together.

Why Marcus.

  1. Reliability behind the wheel

    Eighteen years at Ironbound with one of the strongest attendance records on the board. Pulled out for blizzards, holidays, and short-staffed Sundays without complaint.

  2. Mentor to new operators

    Trains new hires informally on his own time — route quirks, layover etiquette, when to call the steward instead of the supervisor. Every operator who's come through Ironbound in the last decade knows him.

  3. Safety record

    Zero preventable accidents in his career. Recognized by the board for de-escalating an incident on the 25 line that could have gone much worse.
